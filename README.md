# Learn with WAO

This repository archives the resources, tools, courses, and templates from 
[Learn with We Are Open Co-op](https://learnwith.weareopen.coop/).

After 10 years of creative collaboration, We Are Open Co-op (WAO) is closing on
1st May 2026. This repo ensures that everything we built remains free, open, and
accessible long after the website is only available via web archives.

All content is shared under a [Creative Commons CC BY licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
unless otherwise stated.

---

## 📂 What's in this repo

### `/courses`
PDF versions of WAO's free email-based courses, preserved here so they remain
openly available. Courses include:

- **Reframing Recognition** — Going beyond microcredentialing to embrace Open Recognition
- **Feminism is for Everybody, Especially Educators** — An introduction to feminist pedagogy
- **What We Talk About When We Talk About Open** — Helping you and your organisation work more openly
- **How to Unf\*ck Your Organisation** — Untangling organisational spaghetti with actionable steps
- **The 7 Habits of Highly Successful Online Meetings** — Improving your online meeting experience

### `/templates`
Visual template images for WAO's tools and approaches — ready to use in your own
facilitation and organisational work. Templates include:

- Strategic Starfish (retrospective & planning)
- Sailboat Retrospective
- EOY Retrospective
- Audience Ikigai
- Persona Mapping
- Community Canvas
- Opportunity Prioritisation Matrix
- Big Reveal
- Defining the Cast
- Eisenhower Matrix
- Fast 5's
